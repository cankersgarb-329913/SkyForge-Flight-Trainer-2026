## 🎮 About the Game

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian flight simulation game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Microsoft. The base game released on August 18, 2020, and the free 40th Anniversary Edition update launched on November 11, 2022. It features detailed aircraft, global scenery, live weather, real-world air traffic, airports, helicopters, and gliders.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Fuel** — Prevents fuel quantities from decreasing during flight, allowing extended routes without refueling.
- **Freeze Fuel** — Locks the current fuel level at its present value.
- **Aircraft Protection** — Helps prevent aircraft damage caused by hard landings, overstress, crashes, and other simulation events.
- **Engine Assistance** — Reduces the impact of selected engine failure conditions during training and testing.
- **Unlimited Simulation Rate** — Provides convenient control over accelerated simulation time for long flights and route testing.
- **Flight Assistance** — Supports practice sessions by reducing selected penalties and maintaining a more forgiving flight environment.
- **No Collision Penalty** — Prevents collisions from ending a practice session or applying the usual crash consequences.

### 🌍 World

- **Weather Lock** — Locks the current weather conditions so that live or preset weather does not change during the flight.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Slew Mode Support** — Makes it easier to reposition the aircraft while using the simulator's built-in slew controls.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start a flight normally and select the aircraft, departure airport, arrival airport, weather, and time of day. Use the trainer's protection and fuel options after the aircraft has loaded. Microsoft Flight Simulator uses standard flight controls for pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, landing gear, flaps, brakes, autopilot, and trim. The simulator also includes a built-in slew mode for repositioning the aircraft without normal aerodynamic movement.

### Tools & Inventory

The game has no conventional character inventory or item-management system. Fuel is the main persistent resource relevant to this trainer. Unlimited Fuel and Freeze Fuel affect the aircraft's fuel quantity while the aircraft is active; they do not add aircraft, airports, liveries, or downloadable content to the simulator.

### World & Environment

The simulator represents a streamed, globe-spanning environment with airports, terrain, buildings, points of interest, live or preset weather, time-of-day settings, and air traffic. Weather Lock is useful for repeating the same approach or testing aircraft behavior under stable conditions. Changes to the world, weather, and flight plan should be made through the simulator's own menus whenever possible.

### Combat & Weapons

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian flight simulator and has no conventional combat, weapons, or combat inventory system. The relevant trainer functions are aircraft protection and collision assistance, which can help prevent crashes or damage during flight training, aircraft testing, helicopter operations, and glider practice.
